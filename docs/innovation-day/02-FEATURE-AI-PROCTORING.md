# 👁️ Keeping it Fair: Edge AI Proctoring
**"Integrity you can trust, without spying on students."**

## 🛑 The Problem with "Cloud Spying"
Most proctoring today feels creepy. It streams your room, your face, and your life to some server far away. It’s a privacy nightmare, and it’s a bandwidth hog that crashes if your Wi-Fi isn't perfect.

## ✅ The ShadowCoders Way
We decided that the best place to handle proctoring is **right there on the student's own screen.**

### How it Works (Simply)
We don't record video. Instead, we use a lightweight "Edge AI" that runs directly inside the browser. It’s like a silent observer that only notes the important things:
*   **"Are you looking at the screen?"**: It understands head movements in real-time.
*   **"Is there someone else there?"**: It ensures only the student is taking the test.
*   **iris Tracking**: It can even tell if your eyes are darting toward a hidden note.

### Why This Wins
1.  **Strict Privacy**: No video ever leaves the student's laptop. We only send "events" (like "Face Detect: OK").
2.  **No Lag**: Since the "thinking" happens on the computer, it doesn't matter if the internet is slow.
3.  **Universal**: No heavy software to install. If you have a browser, you're ready.
