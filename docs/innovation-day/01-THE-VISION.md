# 🚀 ShadowCoders: Rethinking How We Grade
**"Because great education shouldn't be gated by expensive API bills or privacy risks."**

## 🌟 The Spark
Every exam platform out there today feels like it’s built for profit, not for students or teachers. They charge you for every single essay graded, and they want you to trust them with your private webcam feeds. We thought: **"What if we could build something better, right here on our own hardware?"**

ShadowCoders is our answer. It's an autonomous ecosystem built to live inside an institution’s own walls. We aren't just checking boxes; we're giving control back to the schools.

## 💡 Why We Built This
We saw three big problems that were holding everyone back:
*   **The "AI Tax"**: Why should it cost $500 just to grade a batch of 10,000 student essays? It shouldn't.
*   **The Privacy Gap**: Why does a student need to send their face to a cloud server just to prove they aren't cheating? They shouldn't.
*   **The "Oh No" Factor**: Internet goes down, and suddenly the whole exam is a disaster. It doesn't have to be.

## 🎯 What Makes Us Different?
We aren't just "in the cloud." We are wherever you need us to be. 

*   **Zero-Cost AI**: We use the power already sitting in your computer labs to grade papers for free.
*   **Privacy that actually works**: Our proctoring happens in the browser—it never even sees your server, much less a third-party's. 
*   **Bulletproof Reliability**: We built a system that "remembers" everything. If the power cuts out, your work doesn't.

## 🏁 Our Vision
We’re building more than just an app; we're building a future where intelligence is a utility that anyone can own. No more subscription fees for thinking. Just clean, private, and powerful assessment for everyone.
