## 🚀 What is ShadowCoders?
**ShadowCoders** is a state-of-the-art online assessment and placement-training platform built to bridge the gap between academic preparation and industry demands. Designed specifically with placement drives in mind, it provides a comprehensive environment for students to upskill and for institutions to streamline their hiring processes.

---

## 🎯 Empowering Students & Staff

### 🧑‍🎓 For Students: Upskill & Excel
ShadowCoders isn't just a testing tool—it's a growth platform. It helps students:
*   **Attend rigorous tests** that mirror real-world technical company rounds.
*   **Upskill themselves** through adaptive learning, challenging coding problems, and instant feedback.
*   **Run code seamlessly** using a unique hybrid execution environment that eliminates server lag.
*   **Earn real-world rewards** through a gamified system that encourages continuous improvement.

### 👨‍🏫 For Staff: Filter & Identify Top Talent
Placement officers and staff members are equipped with powerful tools to:
*   **Filter students effortlessly** based on performance, coding efficiency, and specific skill sets required by visiting companies.
*   **Monitor progress in real-time** with advanced analytics and responsive dashboards.
*   **Organize company-specific cohorts** to ensure the right students are shortlisted for the right opportunities.

## 🛡️ Why ShadowCoders is the Best

ShadowCoders stands out from traditional platforms due to its unmatched security, making it **impossible for students to engage in malpractice**. We achieve Proctorio-level security ($0 cost per exam) using cutting-edge, native browser APIs. 

**Proctoring & Anti-Cheating Features:**
*   **AI-Powered Eye & Head Tracking:** Utilizes browser-based MediaPipe Face Landmarker to monitor gaze direction and head movement, instantly detecting if a student looks away or if multiple people are in the frame.
*   **11-Layer Defense System:** Automatically detects tab switching, prevents copy-pasting from external sources, blocks developer tools, and enforces a strict fullscreen mode.
*   **Smart Warning System:** Progressively warns students of violations. If warnings are ignored, the exam is automatically submitted, ensuring absolute integrity.
